About

Don't take me seriously. I do this for fun.

I'm Deshaun Frye, the photographer and filmmaker behind Byrdz Eye. I shoot the people and culture of New Orleans: portraits with character, street scenes after dark, Mardi Gras Indians on Super Sunday, and the energy of Jazz Fest and second lines.

Every frame starts with the person in front of the lens. No stiff poses, just real moments with a cinematic edge.
